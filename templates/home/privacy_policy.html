4. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help the site function correctly. Below is a summary of the cookies we use:

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
sessionid Essential Maintains your login session across pages. 1 week
csrftoken Essential Protects forms against cross-site request forgery attacks. 1 year

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.
